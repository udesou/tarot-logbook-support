Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Tarot Logbook is designed as a private personal logbook. It does not use advertising or analytics SDKs, and it does not sell or share your reading data.

Readings and storage

Your readings, reflections, saved spreads, deck names, and any card photos you add are stored on your device. When iCloud sync is available and enabled for Tarot Logbook, this information can sync through your private iCloud account using Apple’s CloudKit service. Tarot Logbook does not operate a separate account system or a server that can read your private iCloud data.

You can export a backup or Markdown report when you choose. Exported files are handled by the destination you select, such as Files, Mail, or another app.

Photos

If you choose to add a card photo, Tarot Logbook requests access only through Apple’s photo picker or camera flow. The selected image is stored with your app data and, if iCloud sync is active, may sync through your private iCloud account. Tarot Logbook does not upload card photos to its own server.

Card artwork

For Rider-Waite-Smith card images, Tarot Logbook may request public artwork from Wikimedia Commons and cache it on your device. Those requests are subject to Wikimedia’s own privacy practices.

Purchases

Tarot Logbook Premium is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple through the App Store. Payment information is handled by Apple. Tarot Logbook receives only the purchase entitlement information needed to make Premium features available.

Feedback and support

If you choose to send feedback, Tarot Logbook opens your email app with a draft addressed to udesou@gmail.com. Any information you send is handled by your email provider and the recipient of that email.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the current version will be published at this address. For privacy questions, contact udesou@gmail.com.
