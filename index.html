About the app

Tarot Logbook helps you record readings in a form you can revisit: spreads, decks, card pulls, reflections, mood, theme, and who a reading was for. Readings can sync privately through your iCloud account, and you can keep a manual backup whenever you want.

Tarot Logbook Premium is a one-time App Store purchase that unlocks saved custom spreads, personal decks and card photos, Search, Insights, and Markdown export.
