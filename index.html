Private by design

Your readings belong to you.

Tarot Logbook has no developer-operated account or journal server. Your readings stay on your device and, when available, in your private iCloud account. You decide when to create a backup, report or share image.

How your data is handled

Quick answers

Common places to look.

Where can I change themes, decks or spreads?

Open More, choose Customize Journal, then select Themes, Decks or Spreads.

Where are backup and export tools?

Open More, then Data & Backup. You can import or export a backup, replace the journal from a backup, and create a Markdown report.

Can I see the guided tour again?

Yes. Open More, choose Help, then Quick Tour.

How do I restore Premium?

Open More, choose Tarot Logbook Premium, then use Restore Purchases. Make sure the device is signed into the Apple Account used for the original purchase.
